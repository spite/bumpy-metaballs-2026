Isosurface created with Marching Cubes, and bump added with normal mapping.

The metaballs don't have generated UV coordinates, and tangents can't be calculated with the usual method because it relies on texture coordinates.

The triplanar mapping explained in this GPU Gems 3 article supplies them instead: each of the three projections carries its own tangent frame, so the normal map can be applied in the fragment shader with no UVs at all.

Shading is the same MatCap as Spherical Environment Mapping, now with ambient occlusion, bloom and chromatic aberration on top.
